Clear advice before any equipment is chosen.

Solward brings commercial understanding and electrical expertise to solar and energy decisions. We start with your property and how you use power, then work through the options clearly.

Every system is sized against a real roof, a real switchboard and a real power bill. We set out what the numbers assume, what the install involves and where the trade-offs sit, so the decision stays yours to make with the full picture in front of you.

Generate. Store. Supply.

Three parts of one system. Each builds on the last, and you can start at any point.

01

Generate

Solar generation, with a panel layout and inverter designed for your roof, its shading and your daily load rather than a standard package.

02

Store

Battery storage matched to how and when you use power, so the system holds what you generate and covers the hours that matter to you.

03

Supply

The direction we are building towards: owned generation paired with contracted electricity supply, so one relationship covers the whole picture.

Generation and storage are available now. Energy supply is in development and is not yet an offered service.

How a Solward project runs.

Four stages, each with a clear output you can read before the next one starts.

01

Site visit

Roof, orientation, shading, switchboard and meter. We look at your last few bills alongside it.

02

Written proposal

Layout, equipment, scope and price, with every assumption behind an estimate set out in its own section.

03

Install

Scheduled work by a licensed electrician, with the property left tidy and the system commissioned properly.

04

Handover

Monitoring set up, documentation handed over, and a walk through what the first months of data will show.
