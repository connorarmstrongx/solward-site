Privacy

How we handle your details.

What we collect through this website, why, and what you can ask us to do. Last updated 20 October 2026.

What we collect

When you send a proposal brief, we collect what you enter: the property type and address, roof type, your approximate monthly power bill, when the property is busiest, any notes, and your name, email address and phone number if you give one. If you started from the system shaper on the Residential page, we also receive its indicative figures. Nothing is sent until you choose to send your brief.

Why we collect it

To reply to your enquiry, arrange a site visit and prepare a written proposal. We do not sell your details, and we will not use them for unrelated marketing without asking you first.

Who sees it

Your brief is received by Solward. It reaches us through a form service and an email provider that handle it on our behalf. We may share the relevant details with people who work with us on your job when arranging your site visit and proposal. Some of these providers are based overseas, so your information may be processed outside New Zealand.

Visiting this website

We do not currently use advertising trackers or analytics on this site. The site loads its typeface from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads, and our hosting provider keeps standard server logs.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry and any work that follows, then delete or anonymise them.

Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. Email hello@solward.co.nz and we will respond. If you are not happy with how we handle a request, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.
