Privacy policy

How we handle your details.

What Solward collects, why, who sees it, and what you can ask us to do. Last updated 20 October 2026.

Who we are

Solward (“we”, “us”) provides solar and energy advice and installation for homes and businesses in Auckland, New Zealand. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through this website and when you contact us, in line with the Privacy Act 2020.

What we collect

When you request a free quote, we collect what you enter: the property type and address, roof type, your approximate monthly power bill, when the property is busiest, whether you are interested in finance, any package you chose, any notes, and your name, email address and phone number if you give one. If you started from the system shaper, we also receive its indicative figures. When you call or email us, we keep a record of what you tell us. Nothing from the form is sent until you choose to send it.

Why we collect it

To reply to your enquiry, arrange a site visit, prepare a written quote, and, if you go ahead, carry out and support the work. We do not sell your details, and we will not use them for unrelated marketing without asking you first.

Who sees it

Your details are received by Solward. They reach us through a form service and an email provider that handle them on our behalf. We may share relevant details with people who work with us on your job, such as installers and electricians, when arranging your site visit and quote. If you ask about finance, we will only share details with a lender at your request. Some of our providers are based overseas, so your information may be processed outside New Zealand.

Visiting this website

We do not currently use advertising trackers or analytics on this site. The site loads its typeface from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads, and our hosting provider keeps standard server logs. If we add analytics later, we will update this policy first.

How we keep it safe

We limit access to enquiry details to people who need them, and we use reputable providers with appropriate security. No system is perfectly secure. If a privacy breach occurs that is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify affected people and the Privacy Commissioner as the law requires.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry and any work that follows, and to meet our legal obligations, then delete or anonymise them.

Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. You can also ask us to delete your enquiry details. Email hello@solward.co.nz or call 09 215 7434 and we will respond. If you are not happy with how we handle a request or a concern, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

Children

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact us

Our privacy contact can be reached at hello@solward.co.nz or on 09 215 7434.
