These figures are indicative and are not a quote or a savings guarantee. They assume:
  • An Auckland yield of about 1,350 kWh per kW installed each year, before shading.
  • An average retail rate of 32c per kWh and a daily fixed charge of about $1.90.
  • An unshaded, structurally suitable roof with room for the full layout.
  • A battery round-trip efficiency of about 90% and a usable depth of about 90%.
A site visit replaces every one of these with the real numbers for your property.

What a home system includes.

Panel layout

Inverter and switchboard

Inverter sizing, isolation, and any switchboard work needed to bring the installation up to standard.

Battery and backup

Storage sized to your evening load, with the option of a backup circuit set out clearly if you want one.

Monitoring

App-based monitoring configured at handover so you can see generation, use and export from day one.
