About

Towards the sun.

Solward joins sol, the Latin word for sun, with ward, meaning in a direction. The name carries the intent: movement towards the sun, and a more deliberate approach to energy.

A category that can feel technical and transactional.

Solward exists to make solar and energy decisions legible. Clear advice, considered system design, accountable delivery.

For homeowners and businesses making long-term energy decisions, we bring commercial understanding and electrical expertise together, with systems shaped around the property and the way it uses power. We would rather explain a trade-off than sell around it.

Two brothers.

One technically minded, one with business and commercial experience.

The brand started with complementary strengths in one family. One brother brings electrical expertise and technical delivery. The other brings business and commercial experience, with a focus on customers and the commercial case. It means the person who scopes your system and the person who installs it answer to each other, and the work stays visible from the first site visit to commissioning.

The commercial brother

Business and commercial experience. First point of contact, site visits and proposals.

The technical brother

System design, installation and technical sign-off, carried out by a licensed electrician.

How we work.

Four habits that shape every proposal we send.

Clear

Explain the options and the next step in plain language, including the parts that are less convenient.

Assured

Let the design, the scope and the evidence support the claim. No guaranteed savings, no absolutes.

Human

Speak directly to the customer and take responsibility when something needs to be put right.

Practical

Connect every feature back to the way you actually use energy, not to a spec sheet.

Generation and storage today. Energy supply is the direction we are building towards.

We publish a service when it is available, not before. Solar generation and battery storage are what we deliver now; contracted electricity supply is in development.

Our commitment

We stand behind every job.

A solar system is a long-term decision, so we treat the work as ours long after the install is finished. Everything we do is held to the standard we would want on our own roof.

01

In writing, first

Scope, equipment, price and the assumptions behind every estimate are set out before any work is booked.

02

Done properly

Every system is checked, tested and commissioned before handover, and we walk you through how it works.

03

Here after the install

If something is not right, we come back and put it right. You deal with the same people from first visit to follow-up.

04

Plain talk

No guaranteed savings and no pressure. We explain the trade-offs, including the ones that point to a smaller system.
